About BrickMisfit

Still building.

Because growing up never had to mean putting the bricks away.

Hello Builders!

I've been building with bricks since I was a kid. Apparently, I never got the memo that I was supposed to stop.

BrickMisfit is my corner of the internet for sharing the builds I enjoy, the ones that test my patience, the occasional MOC, and probably more tiny plastic pieces than any reasonable person needs.

I especially enjoy the bigger, more challenging sets and the simple process of sitting down, tuning everything else out, and figuring out where the next piece goes.

No speed building. No taking it too seriously. Just enjoying the build, one brick at a time.

Enjoy life, one brick at a time.
