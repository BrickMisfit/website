About BrickMisfit

Still building.

Because growing up never had to mean putting the bricks away.

Hello Builders!

I'm BrickMisfit, and I turn my favorite LEGO builds into relaxing, fun experiences.

I've been building since I was a kid and never really stopped. Today that means bigger sets, more ambitious projects, custom MOCs and, occasionally, less shelf space than I'd like.

BrickMisfit is a place for builds, challenges, ideas, tips and discussion, with room to slow down and simply enjoy creating something.

Enjoy life, one brick at a time.
